Welcome to Inlet

Inlet keeps your Downloads folder clean. It sorts files into folders like Images, Documents and Installers, either automatically as they finish downloading, or when you click one button.

  • Nothing is deleted or overwritten. Inlet only moves files, and you can undo anything with ⌘Z.
  • You stay in control. Inlet starts in Manual mode, so nothing moves until you say so.
  • Find files by describing them, like “pdfs from github last week”.
  • Everything stays on your Mac. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

This installer puts Inlet in your Applications folder. It works on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and takes under a minute.

Inlet is free and open source (MIT license).
