Inlet is installed

Inlet opens by itself in a moment. You’ll find its icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen, and the app in your Applications folder.

  1. When macOS asks whether Inlet can access your Downloads folder, click Allow.
  2. A short tour shows you around. Inlet starts in Manual mode: open Organize to see where each file would go, then click Tidy.
  3. Want new downloads sorted automatically? Switch on Auto mode in the sidebar.

To update later, run the new installer. Your settings and history are kept.
To uninstall, quit Inlet from its menu bar icon and move it from Applications to the Trash.
